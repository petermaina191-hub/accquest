Our story

Formation and skill, on the same campus.

Accquest Training Institute is a Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) institute based on the ACC&S Cathedral grounds, General Kago Street, Thika. We bring together ministry formation and marketable trade skill under one roof.

Mission

Equip learners with knowledge that changes their circumstances.

We train for competence, not just certificates — pairing classroom instruction with hands-on studio, kitchen and lab time so graduates leave ready to work, serve or start a trade of their own.

Vision

A recognised centre of excellence in values-based technical training in Kiambu County.

We measure ourselves by graduate outcomes: certification achieved, placements made, and communities served.

Knowledge

Sound theory behind every practical skill we teach.

Innovation

Training methods that keep pace with what industry actually needs.

Impact

Graduates who go on to employ, serve and lead in their communities.

Integrity

Assessment and conduct that graduates and employers can trust.

Regulation & accreditation

Where we sit within Kenya's TVET system.

Kenya's technical and vocational institutes operate under the TVET Act, with oversight from the Technical and Vocational Education and Training Authority (TVETA) and assessment bodies such as KNEC and the Curriculum Development, Assessment and Certification Council (CDACC). Our Baking Technology Artisan course is assessed under the CDACC framework, as printed on our current prospectus.
