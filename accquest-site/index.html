About Accquest

A TVET institute built around the courses Thika's job market actually needs.

Based on the ACC&S Cathedral grounds along General Kago Street, Thika, Accquest Training Institute pairs values-based formation with employable, hands-on skill. Every programme is delivered by practising trainers and backed by structured assessment under Kenya's Competency-Based Education and Training (CBET) system, so what you learn in class is what employers ask for on the job.
