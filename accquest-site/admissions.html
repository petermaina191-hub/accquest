Admissions.

Follow the steps below, then complete the online application form. Hostel places are limited and allocated in order of confirmed admission.

Process

How to join Accquest.

1. Choose your course

Review Christian Chaplaincy, ICT or Baking Technology on our courses page and confirm you meet the entry requirement.

2. Submit your application

Complete the online form below, or visit our admissions office on the ACC&S Cathedral grounds with your certificates.

3. Receive your admission letter

Our admissions office reviews applications and issues an admission letter with fee and reporting details.

4. Pay fees & report

Clear the confirmed fee balance and report on your intake date. Hostel booking is confirmed at this stage.

What to bring

  • National ID or birth certificate
  • KCPE / KCSE result slip or certificate (as applicable to your course)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Completed application form (online or paper)
  • Application fee receipt [confirm amount]
